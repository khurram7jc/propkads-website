# Propkads — Business Strategy & Operational Architecture

## 1. Core Identity & Business Model
**Propkads** is a specialized **Real Estate Growth & Direct Sales Engine** for developers, housing societies, high-rises, and major commercial projects.

We do NOT operate as a standard vanity agency that charges small retainers for disconnected social media posts. We provide a complete full-funnel system engineered towards **final property bookings and sales closures**:

```
[Brand & 3D Assets] ──► [Performance Paid Ads] ──► [Lead Funnels & WhatsApp] ──► [Sales Alignment & Closures]
```

### Revenue Model
- **Primary Driver:** Project sales commissions & success-driven partnerships (capturing the high-margin sales transaction upside).
- **Secondary Driver:** Complete full-funnel digital marketing & media production retainers (3D modeling, walkthroughs, ad spend management, web systems).

---

## 2. The Role of CRM (Strict Policy)

### Internal Engine (Proprietary Asset)
- The custom **Propkads Real Estate CRM** is our internal secret weapon and operational command center.
- It encodes proprietary sales management workflows, SMC/ICT conversion frameworks, dealer protection, inventory locking, and agent audit metrics.
- **NEVER OFFER AS PUBLIC SAAS OR SUBSCRIPTION:** We do NOT sell or license our proprietary CRM to outside clients or developers. Disclosing or licensing the software means exposing our internal business logic and operational moat.
- **Internal Access Control (RBAC):** Our in-house sales team and agents have strictly segregated, role-based views (agents only access their assigned leads; overall business intelligence and client data remain protected).

### Client-Facing Strategy (Integration Services)
- On the public website, we offer **CRM Setup & Sales Pipeline Integration** as a service.
- When a client developer needs their own CRM setup, we configure and integrate 3rd-party industry solutions (HubSpot, LeadSquared, Zoho, etc.) connected to their ad funnels, keeping our proprietary CRM 100% in-house.

## 3. End-to-End Service Scope
1. **3D Architectural Walkthroughs & CGI:** Master plan flythroughs, elevation renderings, VR tours.
2. **Branding & Print Media:** Luxury investor brochures, society hoardings, billboard layouts, sales office collateral.
3. **Content & Video Production:** On-ground presenter tours, project progress reels, executive interviews.
4. **Drone Videography:** High-definition development progress shoots.
5. **Performance Paid Advertising:** Targeted buyer campaigns across Meta, Google Search/Display, YouTube, and TikTok.
6. **WhatsApp Business API Automation:** Sub-60-second brochure delivery, automated lead qualification.
7. **Web & Landing Page Funnels:** High-speed Next.js project portals, installment calculators.
8. **Sales Pipeline & CRM Integration:** Lead routing, deduplication, and sales rep response accountability.
9. **Direct Sales Closing:** In-house sales force driving qualified buyer site visits and signing booking transactions.
